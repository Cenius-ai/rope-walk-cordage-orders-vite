Rope Walk Cordage Orders

The API behind Ellery Ropewalk: cordage orders carrying their full lay and fibre specification, a run list sequenced longest-first, and the hemp and sisal bale ledger.

  • Orders — fibre, strand count, lay direction, lay length in mm, diameter in mm, finished length in metres.
  • Ropewalk schedule — one click rebuilds the run list from confirmed orders, longest finished length first.
  • Bale stock — hemp and sisal bales in kilograms with per-fibre totals.
